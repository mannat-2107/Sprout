Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective for all Sprout users
Internal Draft — Not for Public Use. This document has not been reviewed by a legal professional. Publish only after lawyer sign-off, especially for Aadhaar, PAN, and DPDP Act sections.
Summary: Sprout does not sell your personal data. We collect only what is strictly required by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) to verify your investor account and execute your investment orders.

1. Information We Collect

To enable mutual fund investment services, we collect and process the following information:

  • Identity Data: Name, Date of Birth, Permanent Account Number (PAN), and gender as recorded in official registries.
  • KYC and Depository Verification: One-time password (OTP) verification records via UIDAI paperless e-KYC and KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs).
  • Banking Information: Bank account number and IFSC code required for penny-drop bank account validation and SEBI-mandated fund routing.
  • Device & Analytics Data: IP address, operating system, and anonymous in-app funnel events to improve latency on entry-level mobile devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  1. Authenticate your identity with SEBI-registered KYC Registration Agencies (CVL, NDML, CAMS, Karvy).
  2. Facilitate transactions directly through BSE Star MF and registered Asset Management Companies (AMCs).
  3. Communicate mandatory transaction receipts, statement updates, and regulatory notices.

3. Data Protection and Storage

All sensitive communications are encrypted using TLS 1.3 with 256-bit encryption. All data is stored within certified cloud servers located within the territory of the Republic of India in full accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and SEBI data localization mandates.
